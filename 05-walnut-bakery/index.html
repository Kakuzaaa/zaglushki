Our story

The Walnut Bakery opened its shutters on Broughton Street in March 2009, in what used to be a print shop. Fiona McCabe, who trained at a boulangerie in Lyon before coming home to Edinburgh, wanted one thing: a neighbourhood bakery where the bread has an ingredient list you can count on one hand.

Seventeen years later, the queue on Saturday mornings still stretches past the barber's next door. Our starter — affectionately known as "Nutkin" — has been fed twice a day, every day, since 2008.

— Fiona & the Walnut bakers
