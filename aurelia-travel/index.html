Frequently Asked Questions.

How does planning work?
Every engagement begins with a private consultation with your assigned Travel Director. We map your culinary, architectural, timing, and aircraft preferences before presenting a preliminary narrative itinerary within 48 hours.
Can journeys be customized?
Yes. Every single element — from departure times and aircraft cabin configurations to specific villa wings and private after-hours museum entries — is customized strictly around your schedule.
Do you arrange private experiences?
Yes. Through our Sovereign Aviation and Maritime partnerships, we coordinate light jets, ultra-long-range aircraft, superyachts, after-hours museum viewings, and private Michelin chef dining.
How far in advance should I book?
For peak seasons (Amalfi Coast in July, Swiss Alps in February, Kyoto cherry blossoms in April), we recommend 3 to 6 months in advance. However, our Aurelia Private members enjoy guaranteed 4-hour jet dispatch and emergency priority villa allocation year-round.
Can you plan multi-country journeys?
Seamlessly. We frequently choreograph multi-continental voyages (e.g. London to Reykjavik, then private flight to Patagonia and Polynesia), managing all diplomatic clearances, luggage handling, and medical-grade security.

Editorial Essay

The art of slow travel

By Aurelia Editorial Staff · Geneva

In an era where travel has been accelerated into a frantic checklist of digital captures, true luxury resides in the deliberate slowing of pulse and perspective. When we choose to stay longer in a single valley, the landscape ceases to be a backdrop and begins to speak.

“The greatest journey is not the one that traverses the most meridians, but the one that leaves the deepest stillness in the soul.”

Our directors select properties not merely for their marble or linens, but for their acoustic quietude, their architectural reverence for native stone, and their ability to restore the timeless rhythm of human thought.
