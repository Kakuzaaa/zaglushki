How does ORION prevent synthetic model hallucinations?
Every insight produced by ORION is grounded in a deterministic knowledge graph with explicit bibliographic provenance. Statements cannot be generated without an audited traversal path directly linking to peer-reviewed literature or patent publications.
What sources are indexed in the global corpus?
We continuously ingest major open-access repositories (arXiv, bioRxiv, ChemRxiv, PubMed), global patent offices (USPTO, EPO, JPO, WIPO), conference proceedings (IEEE, ACM, NeurIPS), and institutional open repositories worldwide.
Can our laboratory ingest proprietary internal research documents?
Yes. Research and Enterprise tiers support isolated encrypted vector stores. Your internal lab notebooks, unpublished drafts, and proprietary data remain strictly confidential and are never used to train global public weights.
How frequently is the knowledge graph updated?
Our streaming ingestion pipeline processes new preprint feeds and patent filings in real-time, executing semantic clustering and node reconciliation within minutes of publication.
Is there an API for programmatic integration into computational notebooks?
Yes. We provide official SDKs for Python and TypeScript with native support for Jupyter, Pandas, and NetworkX export, allowing programmatic graph traversal and automated hypothesis generation in CI/CD scientific pipelines.
What are the compliance and data sovereignty standards?
ORION is SOC 2 Type II certified and compliant with GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 standards. Enterprise tiers offer on-premises air-gapped deployments for sensitive defense and bio-security research.

Multimodal Diffusion Bridges for Large-Scale Molecular Dynamics

Dr. Chen Wei, Dr. Elena Rostova, K. Larsson et al.

Abstract & Synthesis

We present a discrete-time continuous score matching approach that constructs continuous diffusion bridges between quantum ab initio energy surfaces and coarse-grained macromolecular trajectories. Empirical validations across 14 protein folding benchmarks demonstrate a 40x acceleration over traditional molecular dynamics solvers.

DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-9
