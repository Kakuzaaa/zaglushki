Frequently Asked

Got questions? We have answers.

Common questions regarding architecture, billing, migration, and performance.

How does Nexora achieve sub-millisecond cold starts?
Nexora utilizes pre-warmed V8 snapshot isolates and lightweight WebAssembly runtimes rather than booting heavyweight operating system containers. This allows instances to instantiate in under 5ms worldwide.
Can I migrate existing Docker and OCI containers to Nexora?
Yes. Nexora natively ingests standard OCI-compliant container images and compiles them down to optimized microVM sandboxes through our automated build engine.
How does the zero-overhead eBPF observability work?
Rather than injecting monitoring SDKs or sidecars into your user-space application code, our hypervisors read socket and kernel tracepoints via safe eBPF bytecode programs. This yields full telemetry without CPU degradation.
What happens if our API traffic spikes by 100x unexpectedly?
Our Anycast ingress automatically distributes load across neighboring PoPs in under 200 milliseconds, and your compute fleet autoscales horizontally without rate-limiting caps.
Are there egress fees or hidden network transfer costs?
No. Nexora includes generous global egress quotas on all tiers. High-volume custom plans benefit from predictable fixed monthly bandwidth commitments.
