Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How do I start trading on AlphaCryptoTrade?
Create a free account in under two minutes, complete identity verification, deposit funds via bank transfer or crypto, and you're ready to trade. Our onboarding wizard guides you through every step.
Is my data and my capital secure?
Yes. We use post-quantum encryption, hold 98% of assets in cold storage, and undergo quarterly third-party audits. Accounts are protected by multi-factor authentication and biometric login.
What fees does AlphaCryptoTrade charge?
Spot trading starts at 0.10% maker / 0.10% taker, with volume-based discounts down to 0.01%. Deposits are free. Withdrawal fees vary by network and are shown transparently before confirmation.
Can I withdraw my funds at any time?
Absolutely. There are no lock-up periods on standard accounts. Withdrawals to whitelisted addresses are processed within minutes; new addresses clear after a short security hold.
Do you support mobile trading?
Yes. Native iOS and Android apps deliver the full feature set — charting, trading, staking, and portfolio analytics — with biometric login and push notifications for price alerts.
Which countries do you operate in?
AlphaCryptoTrade is available in over 120 countries. Availability of specific products (margin, futures) depends on local regulation. Check our supported regions page or contact support for details.
