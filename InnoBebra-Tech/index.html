FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, processes, and partnership approach.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a wide range of industries including financial services, healthcare, retail and e-commerce, manufacturing, telecommunications, education, government, and energy. Our solutions are adaptable to any sector that requires technology innovation and digital transformation.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A proof-of-concept typically takes 4-6 weeks, while full enterprise implementations range from 3-12 months. We use agile methodology with 2-week sprints to ensure continuous delivery and visibility throughout the project.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects, time-and-materials engagements, and dedicated team arrangements. Each project begins with a discovery phase where we define scope, timeline, and budget. We provide transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive post-deployment support including 24/7 monitoring, maintenance contracts, SLA-backed response times, and dedicated account management. Our support teams ensure your solutions continue to perform optimally long after launch.

How do you ensure data security and compliance?

Security is embedded in every stage of our development process. We are ISO 27001 certified, SOC 2 Type II compliant, and follow GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS standards as required. We conduct regular security audits, penetration testing, and maintain a dedicated security operations center.

Can you work with our existing technology stack?

Yes, we specialize in integrating with existing systems. Our team has expertise across all major technology platforms, cloud providers, and programming languages. We can enhance, extend, or modernize your current stack while ensuring seamless integration with legacy systems.

What makes InnoBebra different from other tech companies?

Our differentiators include: a multidisciplinary team combining deep technical expertise with business acumen, proprietary AI/ML frameworks developed in-house, a proven track record with Fortune 500 clients, commitment to transparent communication, and a results-oriented approach that ties our success to your outcomes.

Do you offer remote or on-site engagement?

We offer both remote and on-site engagement models, as well as hybrid arrangements. Our global presence with offices in San Francisco, London, Singapore, and Berlin allows us to provide local support while leveraging our distributed team's expertise across time zones.

How do you handle intellectual property?

All custom-developed solutions and code created specifically for your project belong to you. We sign comprehensive NDAs and IP assignment agreements before project commencement. Your intellectual property rights are fully protected throughout and after our engagement.

What is your approach to project management?

We follow agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban) with dedicated project managers, regular sprint reviews, and transparent progress tracking through tools like Jira and Confluence. Clients receive weekly status reports and have direct access to team leads throughout the engagement.
