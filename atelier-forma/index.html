Studio

Atelier Forma is a Lisbon-based architecture practice founded in 2014 by architects Elena Marques and Thomas Weber. We work across residential, cultural, and workplace projects throughout Europe, with a focus on buildings that respond honestly to their environment.

Our practice is built on the belief that good architecture arises from careful observation — of light, of material, of the way people inhabit space. We approach each commission with a quiet rigor, seeking clarity in both form and detail.

Approach

01

Context

Every project begins with a careful reading of its surroundings — the scale of neighboring buildings, the rhythm of the street, the quality of local light and landscape. Our designs grow from this dialogue, never imposed upon it.

02

Material

We favor materials that age with dignity — concrete, timber, lime plaster, raw metal. Each is chosen for its tactile and atmospheric qualities, and detailed with precision so that the hand of the maker remains legible.

03

Light

Light is the most fundamental material of architecture. We shape openings, courtyards, and volumes to modulate daylight throughout the day and across the seasons, creating spaces that feel alive and connected to the passage of time.
