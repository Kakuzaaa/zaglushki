About

A small, steady team focused on local properties

Evergreen Property Services is a locally owned company dedicated to keeping commercial buildings in good working order, with a practical, straightforward approach to maintenance.

Since 2011, we have partnered with property owners, facility managers, and tenants to provide reliable maintenance across offices, retail units, and mixed-use buildings. Our team combines hands-on experience with clear communication, so you always know what has been done and what is recommended next.

We focus on long-term relationships rather than one-off call-outs. Many of our clients have worked with us for years, trusting us to look after their buildings as if they were our own. That means consistent technicians, familiar faces, and a genuine understanding of how your property is used day to day.

How we work

Every property is different, so we start with a practical review of your building and existing maintenance routines. From there, we agree on a realistic schedule, response times, and reporting format that fits your team.

You will have a single point of contact for all requests, backed by a small team of technicians who know your site. We keep records of each visit, including photos and notes, so you can track work over time and plan future improvements.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If you have a question that is not covered here, please contact us—we are happy to discuss your specific property.

Do you offer one-off repairs, or only ongoing contracts?
+
We work with both. Many clients start with one-off repairs or small projects and later move to a planned maintenance agreement once they see the value of regular visits. We are happy to discuss what makes sense for your property and budget.
How quickly can you respond to an urgent issue?
+
For existing clients, we provide 24/7 emergency support with agreed response times based on the type of incident. For new clients, we will always do our best to attend quickly and stabilize the situation, then plan follow-up work as needed.
Do you work directly with tenants or only with owners and managers?
+
We typically coordinate with property owners, asset managers, or facility teams, but we are used to working in occupied buildings and communicating with tenants on site. We can follow your preferred communication process and escalation paths.
Are your technicians certified for electrical and HVAC work?
+
Our team handles general maintenance and basic support tasks. For certified electrical and HVAC work, we partner with licensed specialists we know and trust. We coordinate their visits and integrate their work into your overall maintenance plan.
How do you price your services?
+
Pricing depends on the size and complexity of the property, the scope of services, and the level of urgency. For ongoing maintenance, we usually agree on a fixed monthly or quarterly fee with clear inclusions, plus separate rates for larger projects. For one-off work, we provide itemized estimates before starting.
