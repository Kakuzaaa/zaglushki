A tradition of straightforward counsel

Hartwell & Brooks was founded in 1987 by Margaret Hartwell and the late James Brooks, two Lincoln's Inn barristers who believed that good legal advice should be plain-spoken, fairly priced and firmly on the client's side.

Today the firm comprises eleven solicitors and a support team of eight, operating from our offices on Chancery Lane — a stone's throw from the Royal Courts of Justice. We act for owner-managed businesses, property investors, families and private individuals across Greater London and the Home Counties.

We are proud of our retention: more than half of our new instructions come from existing clients or personal recommendation.
